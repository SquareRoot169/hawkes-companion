Tectonic 0.17.0 is included unmodified from the official Windows x86_64 MSVC
release. The release archive SHA-256 was verified against GitHub's published
release-asset digest. See PROVENANCE.json and LICENSE.
Source: https://github.com/tectonic-typesetting/tectonic/tree/tectonic%400.17.0

The local cache holds unmodified TeX/LaTeX packages and Latin Modern/AMS font
resources obtained from Tectonic's default TeX Live support bundle. Copyright
and package license comments are retained in the cached files. These resources
retain their original licenses (including LPPL and the relevant font licenses),
not the application license. Package sources are available through CTAN and
the Tectonic support-bundle project:
https://ctan.org/
https://github.com/tectonic-typesetting/tectonic-texlive-bundles
https://www.latex-project.org/lppl/
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern

The PDF preview uses pypdfium2/PDFium. Its license and third-party notices are
retained in runtime/Lib/site-packages/pypdfium2-5.14.0.dist-info/licenses.

Runtime export explicitly uses cached files only and untrusted-input mode.
Online resource preparation occurred during packaging, not during normal use.
